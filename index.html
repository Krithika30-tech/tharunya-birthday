Happy birthday, THARUNYA!! 🥹❤️

It’s funny to think that when I first met you on the 8th, I was the class leader and you were the new joinee. You seemed so quiet that I actually complimented you for being the quiet one. Look at you now — a certified chatterbox. I would like to file a formal complaint about this character development. 😭

Somewhere along the way, you became so much more than just someone in my class. We started doing almost everything together, sharing daily updates, teasing each other, laughing over lunch, caring for each other, and showing up when life got a little difficult. We have our odd and even moments, and I love that I can be completely myself around you — even when I shout, act dramatic, or realize I’m wrong and come back to say sorry.

And honestly, there’s something I don’t always know how to say: I miss our old closeness sometimes. Not because I’m not happy now, and not because I don’t value what we still have. I just miss those days when we felt so close and everything seemed effortless. Maybe things feel a little different now, and I notice that distance even when you’re still right there.

But I’m genuinely happy that I still get to see you beside me in this college chapter. I’m grateful we still get to laugh, talk, share bits of our lives, and make new memories. I hope we keep finding our way back to each other in all the little ways that make this friendship ours.

So, birthday girl, I hope this year is kind to you. I hope you get the love and support you give other people, reasons to laugh until your stomach hurts, and all the good things you sometimes forget you deserve. Keep being your loud, lovely, slightly irritating self.

You went from the quiet new joinee to one of my favorite people, and I’m really glad our paths crossed. No matter how much life changes, I’ll always be thankful for the story we’ve had so far — and for all the chapters still waiting for us.

With all my love (and a legally required amount of roasting),
YOUR CLASS LEADER-TURNED-CHAOS PARTNER ♡
